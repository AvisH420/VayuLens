# Source-Specific Mitigation Interventions

Evidence-based interventions mapped to the dominant pollution source in an area.
Use the source-attribution shares to pick the interventions that will actually
move the needle in a given grid cell or ward.

## Traffic / vehicular (high NO2, high road density)
- Enforce PUC (Pollution Under Control) checks; impound grossly polluting vehicles.
- Divert or restrict heavy diesel trucks; promote CNG/electric freight.
- Signal-timing and congestion management on chronically jammed corridors.
- Expand and prioritise public transport (metro, CNG/electric buses).
- Odd-even vehicle rationing during severe episodes (GRAP Stage IV decision).

## Industrial (high SO2, proximity to industrial zones)
- Switch industrial units to Piped Natural Gas (PNG) / approved cleaner fuels;
  ban pet coke and furnace oil where notified.
- Enforce online continuous emission monitoring on large stacks.
- Time-of-day operating restrictions for polluting units during episodes.
- Relocate or shut chronically non-compliant units.

## Biomass / stubble / waste burning (high UV aerosol index)
- Ban open burning of municipal solid waste and garden/agricultural residue.
- Promote in-situ crop-residue management (bio-decomposer, Happy Seeder) and
  ex-situ uses to cut stubble burning in surrounding agricultural districts.
- Provide waste-collection so residents do not burn refuse.

## Construction dust (high construction density, high AOD)
- Enforce dust screens, covered material, and water sprinkling / anti-smog guns.
- Wheel-washing and covered transport of debris.
- Halt non-compliant sites; invoke the GRAP construction ban at severe AQI.

## Road / soil dust (high AOD, high wind, unpaved surfaces)
- Mechanised road sweeping and water sprinkling on hotspot roads.
- Pave shoulders and unpaved patches; green open ground to bind soil.

## Prioritisation
Rank interventions by (source share × cell PM2.5 × population exposed). Act first
where a single dominant source overlaps with high concentration and dense
population, and where an enforceable rule already exists.
