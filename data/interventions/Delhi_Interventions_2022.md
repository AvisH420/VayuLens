# Historical Intervention Report — Delhi Winter 2022-23

## Odd-Even Vehicle Rationing (2016, 2019)
The Government of NCT of Delhi implemented odd-even road space rationing during
high-pollution episodes. Independent evaluations found a modest reduction of
approximately 4-6% in PM2.5 during the intervention windows, with the largest
benefit observed in central Delhi traffic corridors. Effectiveness was limited
by exemptions and by meteorological variability.

## Construction Ban under GRAP Stage III (November 2022)
A ban on non-essential construction and demolition was invoked when AQI crossed
420. Post-intervention monitoring showed a measurable decline in coarse
particulate (PM10) at sites within 2 km of major construction clusters within
72 hours, attributed primarily to reduced dust re-suspension.

## Industrial Fuel Switching (2019-2021)
Mandated switching of industries in the NCR from coal and pet-coke to piped
natural gas (PNG) reduced sulphur dioxide and particulate emissions from the
affected industrial clusters. Compliance monitoring under the Air Act, 1981,
combined with closure directions for non-compliant units, was central to the
outcome.

## Stubble Burning Management (2020-2023)
Deployment of bio-decomposer and in-situ crop residue management machinery
reduced the number of farm fire counts in some districts, but transboundary
transport of smoke continued to contribute 15-40% of Delhi's PM2.5 during peak
stubble-burning weeks, highlighting the need for coordinated regional action.
