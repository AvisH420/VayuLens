# National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)

## Objective
The National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) is a long-term, time-bound national
strategy launched in 2019 to tackle air pollution across India in a
comprehensive manner. The original target was a 20-40% reduction in
concentrations of PM2.5 and PM10 by 2024, taking 2017 as the base year. The
target was subsequently revised to a 40% reduction in PM10 concentration by
2025-26 for cities under the programme.

## Non-Attainment Cities
NCAP covers 131 non-attainment cities and million-plus cities that did not meet
the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) over a five-year period.
Each city is required to prepare and implement a city-specific Clean Air Action
Plan addressing sources such as road dust, vehicular emissions, industrial
emissions, biomass burning, and construction dust.

## City Action Plan Components
City action plans must include measures for control of road and construction
dust, augmentation of public transport, greening and plantation, management of
municipal solid waste to prevent open burning, and strengthening of the
air-quality monitoring network. Funds are released to cities based on
performance in reducing particulate matter, assessed annually.

## Monitoring and Institutional Framework
NCAP is implemented by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) under the
Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. Source apportionment
studies and emission inventories are required to guide targeted interventions
in each non-attainment city.
