# Construction & Demolition (C&D) Dust Control Rules

Construction and demolition activity is a major source of PM10 and PM2.5 in
Indian cities. Dust control is governed by the Construction & Demolition Waste
Management Rules, 2016, CPCB dust-mitigation guidelines, and GRAP.

## Mandatory dust-control measures at C&D sites
- Sites larger than 20,000 m² must obtain environmental clearance and file a
  dust-mitigation plan.
- Erect wind-breaking barriers / dust screens (minimum height) around the site
  perimeter and around building under construction.
- Cover all construction material (sand, cement, aggregate) and any loose soil;
  store fine material under cover or keep it wetted.
- Cover trucks carrying construction material and C&D waste; provide
  wheel-washing facilities at exit points to prevent track-out of mud onto roads.
- Sprinkle water regularly on the site, on loose earth, and on demolition debris
  to suppress dust; use anti-smog guns on large sites.
- Use covered chutes for debris removal; do not drop debris from height.
- Pave internal roads and material-handling areas; do not allow open dumping.

## Enforcement and penalties
- Urban local bodies and state pollution control boards inspect sites and can
  impose environmental compensation for non-compliance.
- Under GRAP Stage III and IV, construction and demolition is banned outright in
  Delhi-NCR (with exemptions for railways, metro, airports, defence, and
  healthcare), regardless of individual site compliance.
- Repeated violations can lead to a stop-work order and revocation of clearances.

## Attribution note
When source attribution assigns a high "construction" share to a cell, the
appropriate actions are dust-screen and water-sprinkling enforcement, halting
non-compliant sites, and — at severe AQI — invoking the GRAP construction ban.
