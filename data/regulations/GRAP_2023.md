# Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) — Delhi-NCR

## Stage I — Poor (AQI 201-300)
Under GRAP Stage I, mechanized sweeping and water sprinkling of roads shall be
enforced. Strict enforcement of dust control norms at construction and
demolition (C&D) sites above 500 sq. m. is mandatory. Ban on open burning of
solid waste and biomass. Enforce PUC (Pollution Under Control) norms for
vehicles and target visibly polluting vehicles.

## Stage II — Very Poor (AQI 301-400)
Under GRAP Stage II, diesel generator sets shall be restricted except for
essential and emergency services. Parking fees shall be increased to discourage
private transport, and CNG/electric bus and metro services augmented.
Intensify inspections at identified air-pollution hotspots.

## Stage III — Severe (AQI 401-450)
Under GRAP Stage III, there is a ban on construction and demolition activities
in the entire NCR, except for essential projects such as railways, metro,
airports, and national security. Non-essential C&D including excavation,
boring, and drilling is prohibited. Brick kilns and hot mix plants not using
approved cleaner fuels shall be closed. Restrictions on BS-III petrol and
BS-IV diesel light motor vehicles may be imposed. Primary schools may shift to
hybrid classes.

## Stage IV — Severe Plus (AQI above 450)
Under GRAP Stage IV, closure of all non-essential highly polluting industries
operating on unapproved fuels is mandatory. Entry of trucks into the NCR is
banned except those carrying essential commodities or running on LNG/CNG/BS-VI
diesel or electric. Public, municipal, and private offices may operate at 50%
capacity with work-from-home for the rest. Construction and demolition is
completely halted, including for essential public projects. State governments
may take a decision on closure of schools and colleges.
