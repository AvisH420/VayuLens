# Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) — Delhi NCR

The Graded Response Action Plan is a set of emergency measures notified by the
Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) that kick in as air quality
worsens across Delhi and the National Capital Region. Actions are staged by
the Air Quality Index (AQI).

## Stage I — Poor (AQI 201–300)
- Enforce dust-control rules at construction and demolition (C&D) sites.
- Ban open burning of solid waste and biomass.
- Ensure regular mechanised sweeping and water sprinkling on roads.
- Enforce Pollution Under Control (PUC) norms strictly on vehicles.
- Ban the use of diesel generator sets except for essential/emergency services.

## Stage II — Very Poor (AQI 301–400)
- Increase parking fees to discourage private vehicle use.
- Augment CNG/electric bus and metro services.
- Halt construction where dust mitigation is not being followed.
- Intensify road sweeping and water sprinkling on identified hotspot roads.
- Regulate use of diesel generator sets.

## Stage III — Severe (AQI 401–450)
- Ban construction and demolition activities in Delhi-NCR, except railways,
  metro, airports, national-security and healthcare projects.
- Close stone crushers and mining/associated activities.
- Impose restrictions on BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel light motor vehicles
  in Delhi and neighbouring districts.
- State governments may suspend physical classes for younger children.

## Stage IV — Severe Plus (AQI above 450)
- Ban entry of truck traffic into Delhi except those carrying essential
  commodities or running on CNG/electric.
- Ban Delhi-registered diesel medium and heavy goods vehicles, except for
  essential goods.
- Halt all construction and demolition, including highway, road, flyover,
  power line, pipeline and other public-project work.
- State governments to decide on closure of schools and offices, work from
  home, and odd-even vehicle rationing.

GRAP measures for a stage are invoked pre-emptively based on forecasts and
remain in force until air quality improves and stays in a lower band.
