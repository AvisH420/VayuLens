# CPCB Health Advisory for Air Quality Index (AQI) Categories

## AQI Categories and General Guidance
- Good (0-50): Minimal impact. Air quality is satisfactory for all.
- Satisfactory (51-100): Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
- Moderate (101-200): Breathing discomfort to people with lung and heart
  disease. Sensitive groups should reduce prolonged outdoor exertion.
- Poor (201-300): Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
- Very Poor (301-400): Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Avoid
  outdoor physical activity.
- Severe (401-500): Serious health impact even on healthy people; serious
  effect on those with existing diseases.

## Advisory for Sensitive Groups
Children, pregnant women, the elderly, and persons with pre-existing
respiratory or cardiac conditions should avoid all outdoor physical activity
when AQI is in the Very Poor or Severe category. Keep windows closed during
peak pollution hours and use N95/N99 masks if going outdoors is unavoidable.

## Advisory for Outdoor Workers
Outdoor workers, including traffic police and construction labourers, should be
provided N95 masks and rotated to reduce continuous exposure when AQI is Poor
or worse. Employers should reschedule strenuous outdoor work to lower-pollution
hours where feasible.

## Advisory for Schools and Hospitals
Schools should suspend outdoor assemblies and sports when AQI exceeds 300, and
consider closure or hybrid classes when AQI exceeds 400 as advised by
authorities. Hospitals should anticipate an increase in respiratory and cardiac
admissions and ensure adequate stock of nebulisers, oxygen, and medication
during severe air-quality episodes.
