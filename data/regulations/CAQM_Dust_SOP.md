# CAQM SOP for Dust Mitigation and Construction & Demolition (C&D) Sites

## Applicability
This Standard Operating Procedure issued by the Commission for Air Quality
Management (CAQM) in NCR and Adjoining Areas applies to all construction and
demolition project sites, including linear and building projects, within the
National Capital Region.

## Mandatory Dust Control Measures
1. Erection of wind-breaking barriers of at least 6 feet height around the
   project boundary before commencement of construction.
2. Mandatory dust screens/green nets covering the entire building under
   construction.
3. Water sprinkling on exposed soil, haulage roads, and excavation areas, with
   adequate frequency during dry and windy conditions.
4. Covered storage and transportation of construction materials, sand, and
   debris; no material shall be stored on public land or roads.
5. Wheel-washing facilities at every exit of the construction site to prevent
   track-out of mud and dust onto public roads.
6. Installation of anti-smog guns at sites of area 5,000 sq. m. and above.

## Monitoring and Registration
All C&D sites of built-up area 500 sq. m. and above must register on the
designated web portal and install self-monitoring dust sensors linked to the
regulatory dashboard. Non-compliant sites are liable for environmental
compensation and closure directions under the Air Act, 1981 and the
Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.
